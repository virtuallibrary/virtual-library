Code of Conduct

Room & Competition Regulations

Zero tolerance for inactivity, dark frames, or unauthorized unmuting.

Daily Google Meet Rules

24/7 Monitored

The study room operates entirely on silence and visual desk focus. Participants failing camera checks are promptly removed by active session admins.

Save Meet Rules Infographic as:
assets/meet-rules.jpg
  • Desk & Hands Angle: Camera must clearly frame your study table, notes, or workstation. Dark screens or ceiling angles are removed without warning.
  • Permanent Audio Mute: Microphones must never be unmuted. All queries and break notices are posted in the Telegram channel.
  • Break Accountability: Stepping away without tagging a break status in chat invalidates recorded hours for that block.

Competition Regulations

Squad Battles

Competition seasons have stringent verification. Squad points are deducted collectively for unannounced dropouts or fake timers.

Save Competition Infographic as:
assets/competition-rules.jpg
  • Baseline Threshold: Every registered squad member must log between 5 to 10 weekly hours minimum to prevent squad point deductions.
  • Reserve Replacements: Unannounced absences lead to instantaneous replacement by waitlisted applicants.
  • Audit Penalties: Admin deductions apply daily. Manipulating desk cameras or screen sharing results in permanent disqualification.
